# Project Manifesto

## 1. Core Philosophy & Mission Statement

The foundational principle of the All24 application is the creation of a tangible, fluid, and
expressive interface for discovering curated content. The project's mission is to provide an
antidote to sterile, list-based Browse by transforming navigation into a tactile and visually
engaging experience.

Where contemporary apps often rely on simple taps and vertical lists, All24 uses a dual-axis,
multi-browse carousel grid as its primary interaction model. This approach treats content categories
and items not as a database to be queried, but as a physical space to be explored. The application's
core purpose is to guide the user toward discoveries through intuitive, gesture-driven navigation
that prioritizes motion and a sense of place over raw informational density.

## 2. Key Goals

To clarify the project's direction, it is crucial to define its goals:

* **A Curated Canon:** The application is a curated, not encyclopedic, collection of places, events,
  and happenings. The content is the core of the experience.
* **Expressive Navigation:** The primary interface will be a gesture-driven, physics-based carousel
  that embodies the principles of Material 3 Expressive design. Discovery is meant to be a fluid and
  satisfying interaction.
* **Not a Social Network:** While users have identities and can contribute, the application avoids
  features common to social networks. Interactions are mediated through content, not between users
  directly.
* **Minimalist Detail:** Detail screens provide focused information, avoiding the cognitive overload
  of unrelated links, ads, or navigation paths.

## 3. Interface & Design Principles

The application's interface design is governed by a strict set of rules derived from the core
philosophy:

* **Primacy of the Carousel:** The main screen, `MainCarouselScreen`, is the heart of the
  application. The dual-axis carousel is the primary and exclusive method for Browse content
  categories. Text-based search bars are explicitly forbidden as a primary discovery tool.
* **Motion as Meaning:** All transitions and micro-interactions follow M3 Expressive principles. The
  motion of the carousel and the choreographed transitions between screens are not decorative; they
  are fundamental to making the interface feel responsive and intuitive.
* **Gated Interaction:** Access to features that allow content creation or modification is strictly
  gated based on the user's authentication state and role. This reinforces the value of contribution
  and creates a clear distinction between passive consumption and active participation.
