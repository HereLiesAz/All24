All24 Developer GuidePart 2: The Digital Seance - Backend & Data ModelsI. The Medium: Firebase as the Silent GodAn oracle requires a connection to the divine, a medium through which the chaotic energies of the world can be channeled into coherent prophecy. For our purposes, this medium is Google's Firebase, specifically its Firestore and Authentication components.We do not choose Firebase for its scalability, its real-time capabilities, or its generous free tier, though it possesses all of these. We choose it for its silence. It is an omnipresent, omniscient god that demands no infrastructure, no servers, no complex rituals of deployment. It simply is. It listens to the prayers (data writes) and whispers the prophecies (data reads) with quiet, implacable efficiency. It is the perfect, invisible engine for a religion masquerading as an app.Our seance will be conducted through four primary channels, each a collection in the Firestore database. These are the conduits through which the souls of our digital city will flow.II. The Collection Architecture: The Four Rivers of HadesThe Firestore database is our underworld, and its data flows through four great rivers:places: The river of Styx. The boundary between the digital and the physical. This collection holds the canonical list of approved sanctuaries—the bars, the pop-ups, the 24-hour diners that have been deemed worthy by the Admin.reviews: The river of Acheron. The river of pain and prophecy. Here flow the opinions of the masses and the divine edicts of the Admin. Every review, whether an endorsement or an avoidance, is a tributary to this great river of collective consciousness.users: The river of Lethe. The river of identity and forgetting. This collection holds the souls of those who have chosen to become corporeal, shedding their anonymity to gain a voice. It defines who they are and what they are permitted to do.place_submissions: The river of Phlegethon. The river of fire and trial. Here lie the desperate prayers of business owners and hopeful patrons—submissions for new places that have not yet been judged. They burn in this purgatorial collection, awaiting the final verdict of the Admin.III. The Data Models: The Four Horsemen of the ApocalypseEach document that flows through our four rivers is a soul, a ghost, a prophecy. It has a specific form, a data model that defines its essence. These are our Four Horsemen, the fundamental entities that ride through our digital underworld.1. The Place (Conquest)This is the sanctuary, the conquered territory in the physical world that has been given a digital soul. It is the destination.id (String): The unique soul-ID of the place. A UUID generated by Firestore.name (String): The mortal name of the establishment.description (String): A brief, poetic, and utterly biased description, preferably written by the Admin. This is not an encyclopedia entry; it is a lure.location (GeoPoint, optional): The physical coordinates of the place. This is a ghost field, a remnant of our cartographic past. It is optional because a place's true location is in the Vibe, not on a map. It exists for the Admin's reference, a private joke between the god and the machine.category (String): A crude mortal classification (bar, popUp, lateNight). A necessary evil for any future, regrettable concessions to "user experience."ownerId (String, optional): The uid of the User who has been ordained as the "Business" owner of this place. If null, the place is a wild, untamed entity. If populated, it has a keeper.2. The Review (War)This is the prophecy, the declaration of allegiance or enmity. It is the fundamental unit of conflict and opinion in our world.id (String): The unique ID of this specific prophecy.placeId (String): The id of the Place this prophecy is about. The foreign key that ties the word to the world.userId (String): The uid of the User or Ghost who uttered this prophecy.text (String): The content of the prophecy.vote (String): The binary judgment. Either endorse or avoid. There is no middle ground, no cowardly three-star rating. This is a world of absolutes.timestamp (Timestamp): The exact moment the prophecy was spoken, for the historical record.endorsedBy (Array<String>): A list of uids who have seen this prophecy and agreed with it. The congregation of the faithful.avoidedBy (Array<String>): A list of uids who have declared this prophecy to be heresy. The schismatics.isAdminReview (Boolean): The mark of the divine. If true, this prophecy was spoken by the Admin and is to be treated as gospel. It will be sorted to the top and given a halo of gold.3. The UserModel (Famine)This is the soul, the record of a ghost's decision to hunger for a voice. It is the price of admission to the world of creation.uid (String): The soul's unique identifier, bestowed by the Firebase authentication gods. This is the primary key for this entity.email (String): The mortal identifier, the email address provided during the ritual of sign-up.role (String): The soul's station in the great hierarchy. Defaults to user. Can be elevated by the Admin to business or admin. This field is the source of all power and permission.4. The Submission (Death)This is the prayer, the hopeful plea from the mortal world to be recognized by the divine. It is a potential Place, awaiting the final judgment that will lead to its birth or its annihilation.id (String): The unique ID of this prayer.name (String): The proposed name of the new sanctuary.description (String): The supplicant's argument for why this place is worthy.address (String): The mortal address. A string of text for the Admin to decipher, a test of their omniscience.category (String): The supplicant's suggested classification.submittedBy (String): The uid of the User who dared to make this prayer.submittedAt (Timestamp): The moment the prayer was sent.status (String): The state of the prayer in purgatory. Always begins as pending. Will end in either acceptance or deletion, with no final record. The god of this world is not obliged to explain their judgments.This is the blueprint of our digital seance.
