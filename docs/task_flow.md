# Task Flow

This document describes the user's journey through the All24 application, from discovery to contribution.

## Discovery

The user's journey begins on the home feed, where they are presented with a visually rich, endlessly scrollable feed of "Top 24" lists. This design encourages a proactive and serendipitous discovery process, allowing the user to browse, explore, and be surprised.

## Exploration

When a user taps a list item, they are taken to the detail screen, where they can explore a multi-faceted portrait of the establishment. This includes the official "All24 Take," "Creator Takes," "The Vitals," "The People's Voice," and "Know Before You Geaux."

## Contribution

The user can contribute to the All24 community through the "Vibe Check" system. This includes:

*   **Vouching:** A simple, one-tap method to endorse their favorite places.
*   **Commenting:** A guided comment system that encourages witty, specific, and helpful feedback.

Users whose comments are selected to be featured in the "People's Voice" section are awarded a "Tastemaker" badge, which creates a virtuous cycle of high-quality, user-generated content.
