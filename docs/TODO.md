# TODO (Production Roadmap)

This document outlines the granular, step-by-step action plan for transforming the All24 concept into a production-ready mobile application. The plan is divided into six distinct phases.

## Phase I: Pre-Production & Strategy (Weeks 1-4)

1.  **Team Assembly & Roles Definition:**
    *   **Action:** Recruit and onboard the core team: Project Manager, Lead Android Developer, Backend Developer, UI/UX Designer, and Head Curator/Editor.
    *   **Deliverable:** A defined organizational chart with clear roles and responsibilities.
2.  **Business & Legal Formalization:**
    *   **Action:** Establish the legal business entity (e.g., LLC). Secure initial funding. Draft essential legal documents, including Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), a Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service.
    *   **Deliverable:** A legally registered company with secured seed funding and finalized core legal documents.
3.  **Monetization Strategy Finalization:**
    *   **Action:** Detail the hybrid monetization model, including "Branded Lists," a commission structure for creator-driven promotions, and a "Premium Features" tier.
    *   **Deliverable:** A comprehensive monetization plan document.
4.  **Technical Stack & Architecture Definition:**
    *   **Action:** Confirm the technology stack (Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Azure/AWS, Firebase, etc.). Finalize data models and API structure.
    *   **Deliverable:** A technical architecture document.
5.  **Product Roadmap & MVP Scope:**
    *   **Action:** Prioritize all conceptual features to define a Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
    *   **MVP Feature Set:**
        *   User account creation and profile.
        *   Scrollable home feed of "Top 24" lists.
        *   Expandable list items with "The All24 Take" and "The Vitals."
        *   The "Vibe Check" system: "Vouch" button and prompted comment submission.
        *   Curator-managed "People's Voice" section.
    *   **Deliverable:** A detailed product roadmap document.

## Phase II: Design & Prototyping (Weeks 5-8)

1.  **Wireframing:**
    *   **Action:** Create low-fidelity, grayscale wireframes for every screen in the MVP scope.
    *   **Deliverable:** A complete set of wireframes.
2.  **UI/UX High-Fidelity Design:**
    *   **Action:** Develop high-fidelity mockups and a comprehensive design system in Figma based on the established visual language.
    *   **Deliverable:** A complete UI kit and high-fidelity mockups.
3.  **Interactive Prototyping & Motion Design:**
    *   **Action:** Build a clickable, interactive prototype, animating key transitions.
    *   **Deliverable:** An interactive prototype for stakeholder and user testing.
4.  **Prototype User Testing:**
    *   **Action:** Recruit 5-10 target users to test the prototype and gather qualitative feedback.
    *   **Deliverable:** A user testing report and a list of required design revisions.

## Phase III: Development & Implementation (Weeks 9-20)

1.  **Environment & DevOps Setup (Sprint 0):**
    *   **Action:** Configure development, staging, and production environments. Set up Git and a CI/CD pipeline.
    *   **Deliverable:** A fully configured development pipeline.
2.  **Backend Development (Sprints 1-6):**
    *   **Action:** Build the server-side application, APIs, and admin panel/CMS.
    *   **Deliverable:** A fully functional and documented RESTful API.
3.  **Android App (Frontend) Development (Sprints 1-6):**
    *   **Action:** Develop the native Android application in Kotlin using Jetpack Compose, implementing the theme, UI, and all MVP features.
    *   **Deliverable:** A feature-complete Android application connected to the staging API.

## Phase IV: Content & Community Ignition (Weeks 9-20)

1.  **Initial Content Seeding:**
    *   **Action:** The Head Curator begins creating the initial batch of 10-15 "Top 24" lists.
    *   **Deliverable:** A populated content database for launch.
2.  **Creator Program Recruitment:**
    *   **Action:** Identify, outreach, and sign partnership agreements with an initial cohort of 5-10 "Founding Creators."
    *   **Deliverable:** A signed cohort of creators ready for launch week.
3.  **Pre-Launch Marketing Campaign:**
    *   **Action:** Launch a "coming soon" landing page and begin posting teaser content on social media.
    *   **Deliverable:** An active social media presence and a pre-launch email list.

## Phase V: Testing & Go-to-Market (Weeks 21-24)

1.  **Alpha & Beta Testing:**
    *   **Action:** Conduct internal alpha testing, followed by a closed beta test with 50-100 users.
    *   **Deliverable:** A stable, polished release candidate build.
2.  **Go-to-Market (GTM) Execution:**
    *   **Action:** Prepare the Google Play Store listing, a press kit, and coordinate a synchronized content push with creators.
    *   **Deliverable:** A comprehensive and executed launch plan.
3.  **Deployment & Launch Day:**
    *   **Action:** Submit the app to the Google Play Store, and upon approval, publish it. Announce the launch across all channels.
    *   **Deliverable:** All24 is live and available for download.

## Phase VI: Post-Launch, Monitoring & Iteration (Ongoing)

1.  **Performance Monitoring & Maintenance:**
    *   **Action:** Continuously monitor analytics, server performance, and crash reports. Schedule regular maintenance.
2.  **Community Management & Content Cadence:**
    *   **Action:** Curators actively engage with the community and maintain a regular schedule for publishing new content.
3.  **KPI Tracking & Analysis:**
    *   **Action:** Track KPIs (DAU, MAU, retention, etc.) to inform future development.
4.  **Iterative Development:**
    *   **Action:** Begin the development cycle for version 1.1 based on the roadmap and user feedback.
