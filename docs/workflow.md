# All24 Content Workflow

The All24 content engine is powered by a symbiotic ecosystem of three distinct groups: Curators, Creators, and the Crowd (our users). This document outlines the content workflow for each group.

## The All24 Curators: The Editors-in-Chief

The in-house Curators are the guardians of the app's brand and the arbiters of its quality.

### Curator Workflow

1.  **Content Strategy and Creation:**
    *   Curators conceptualize, research, and write the flagship "Top 24" lists that form the core of the app's content.
    *   They also write "The All24 Take" for each featured establishment, ensuring a consistent, high-quality editorial voice.
    *   This content is created and managed through a backend admin panel/CMS.
2.  **Community Management:**
    *   Curators actively monitor user-submitted comments from the "Vibe Check" system.
    *   They identify the most insightful, witty, and helpful contributions to feature in the "People's Voice" section of a venue's Detail View.
3.  **Creator Program Management:**
    *   Curators are responsible for identifying, vetting, and inviting local influencers to join the All24 Creator Program.
    *   They maintain the relationship with these creators, providing guidance and feedback to ensure their content aligns with the All24 brand.

## The Creator Program: Empowering Local Influencers

The invite-only Creator Program integrates the unique voices of the city's best local food influencers directly into the platform.

### Creator Workflow

1.  **Onboarding:** Once a Creator is invited and onboarded, they are given a "Verified" status and access to the "Creator Takes" tool.
2.  **Content Creation:** Creators can upload their own content—short-form videos, photo carousels, and brief written hot-takes—directly to the detail pages of establishments featured on the All24 lists.
3.  **Editorial Collaboration:** Creators have a direct line of communication to the All24 Curators, allowing them to pitch ideas for new lists or suggest under-the-radar venues for consideration.

## The User's Role: From Consumer to Contributor

The everyday user is elevated from a passive consumer to an active and valued contributor through the "Vibe Check" review model.

### User Workflow

1.  **Experience a Venue:** The user visits an establishment featured in the app.
2.  **Leave a "Vibe Check":** The user navigates to the venue's Detail View and leaves their feedback:
    *   They can tap the **"Vouch"** button to endorse the place.
    *   They can leave a comment by filling in the creative, Mad Libs-style prompts.
3.  **Get Featured:** The user's comment is reviewed by the Curators. If it is selected, it will be featured in "The People's Voice" section, and the user will receive a "Tastemaker" badge on their profile.
4.  **Engage with Content:** Users can also engage with the app's content by:
    *   Bookmarking their favorite lists.
    *   Following their favorite Creators to receive notifications about new "Takes."

This multi-layered workflow ensures a constant stream of high-quality, authentic, and engaging content that makes All24 the definitive insider's guide to New Orleans.
