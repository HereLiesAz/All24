# Community Conduct

The All24 community is built on a foundation of respect, authenticity, and a shared love for the New Orleans culinary scene. To maintain a high-quality and trustworthy environment, we have established the following guidelines for conduct.

## The "Vibe Check" System

All24 has replaced the traditional five-star rating system with the "Vibe Check," a qualitative feedback system designed to generate more meaningful and useful community input.

*   **The "Vouch":** Instead of a numerical score, users can "Vouch" for a place they love. This is a simple, powerful endorsement that creates a clear and transparent measure of community love.
*   **Prompt-Driven Comments:** When leaving a comment, users are guided by creative, Mad Libs-style prompts to encourage specific, evocative, and helpful feedback.

## The "Tastemaker" Badge

Users whose comments are selected by the editorial team to be featured in the "People's Voice" section are awarded a "Tastemaker" badge on their profile. This mark of distinction rewards wit, insight, and helpfulness, and encourages a higher standard of discourse within the community.

By participating in the All24 community, you agree to provide thoughtful, constructive, and respectful feedback. We are committed to fostering a positive and engaging environment for all users.
