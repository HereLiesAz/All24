# Screens

This document provides a detailed breakdown of each screen in the All24 application, focusing on the core user experience of discovery and exploration.

## The Home Feed (Lists Screen)

The fundamental departure from traditional review platforms is immediately evident upon launching the app. The home screen is not a search bar, but a visually rich, endlessly scrollable feed of "Top 24" lists. This design choice reframes the user's interaction from one of query to one of discovery.

*   **Interface:** A single, uncluttered column of list item "covers" or "cards."
*   **Philosophy:** The experience is less like searching a directory and more like flipping through the pages of a beautifully designed, opinionated magazine.

### The Anatomy of a List Item Card

Each of the 24 entries on a list is a clean, elegant "card" that reveals layers of rich content upon interaction.

*   **Collapsed State (Cover):** A stunning, full-bleed photograph, the name of the place, its rank (e.g., "No. 7 of 24"), and a single, witty, Zagat-style summary line.

## The Detail Screen

Tapping a card triggers a fluid, seamless animation that expands it into a full-screen "article." This is where the app's hybrid voice comes to life.

### Anatomy of the Detail Screen

*   **The All24 Take:** A 150-word official review, written by in-house curators in the app's signature authoritative and witty style.
*   **Creator Takes:** A horizontal carousel showcasing short-form video clips and photo galleries from verified local influencers.
*   **The Vitals:** All necessary utility information in a clean, easily scannable format:
    *   Address (with one-tap link to maps)
    *   Hours of operation (with a special animated icon for 24-hour spots)
    *   Phone number
    *   Direct link to the menu
*   **The People's Voice:** A curated selection of three to four of the most insightful, witty, or helpful user comments, presented as elegant pull-quotes.
*   **"Know Before You Geaux":** A dedicated section for practical, insider tips (e.g., "Cash only," "The line gets long after 10 PM").

## The Map Screen

A secondary discovery tool. While the app prioritizes curated discovery via lists, this view provides a more traditional map-based interface for users who have a specific location in mind and want to see what All24-vetted spots are nearby.

## The Profile Screen

This screen is designed to be a user's personal culinary scrapbook of their New Orleans journey. It fosters long-term engagement and a personal connection to the platform.

### Anatomy of the Profile Screen

*   A visually engaging grid of all the places the user has "Vouched" for.
*   A collection of their "Tastemaker" comments that have been featured by the curators.
*   Any lists they have bookmarked for future reference.
