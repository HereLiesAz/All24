# All24 Creator Program

## Overview

The All24 Creator Program is an exclusive partnership with verified food and culture influencers in New Orleans. Creators are a vital part of our content ecosystem, providing unique and authentic "Top 24" lists that showcase the best of the city's culinary scene.

## Benefits of Joining

- **Reach a new audience:** Share your content with a dedicated community of food lovers in New Orleans.
- **Monetize your content:** Earn revenue through our partnership program.
- **Exclusive access:** Get invited to special events, restaurant openings, and more.
- **Be a part of the All24 community:** Collaborate with other creators and be a part of the future of food discovery in New Orleans.

## How to Apply

To apply to become an All24 Creator, please email us at [creators@all24.app](mailto:creators@all24.app) with the following information:

- A link to your primary social media presence (Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
- A brief introduction to your content and why you'd be a good fit for All24
- A sample "Top 24" list idea

Our team will review your application and get back to you within 5-7 business days.
